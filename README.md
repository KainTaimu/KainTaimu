### Hello,

I'm a 2nd year student at MacEwan University studying Computer Science. 

I love C# — with most of my experience coming from game development with Godot, and making tools for myself. I also often use Go for small tools.

The projects that show the best of my abilities are: 
- Performance | [SurvivorsGame](https://github.com/KainTaimu/SurvivorsGame-Public) 

A survivors-like game made with Godot and written in C#; you get paid as a contractor to destroy massive hordes of the undead. I made use of data-oriented programming through the ECS library [Arch](https://github.com/genaray/Arch) to support 50× more enemies than the genre-defining game it is inspired by. Being the largest and most complex codebase I have written — about 10K LoC — it also served as valuable experience on how to work with and manage such a codebase without resulting in spaghetti code.

- Code architecture | [transcribe-cs](https://github.com/KainTaimu/transcribe-cs)

A tool that transcribes, translates, and embed subtitles into video/audio. I wrote it to practice clean code and make use of design patterns such as the composite, observer, and factory patterns. As a result, the architecture allows for easy swapping and extending of transcription and translation providers with minimal refactoring.
